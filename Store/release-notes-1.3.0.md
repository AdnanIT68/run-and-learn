# Release notes 1.3.0 (versionCode 6)

Paste each block into the matching language in Play Console (max 500 characters each).
Before this release: the Arabic words must be checked by a native speaker (`Store/arabic-review.md`)
and the build tried on a phone (diploma sharing too).

<en-US>
- New: Arabic words for Bosnian-speaking children, with Latin letters below
- Up to 4 children on one phone, each with their own progress
- Mini-games, letter tracing with a finger and sums up to ten
- New worlds: Kind Words, and Colours and Shapes
- The story of Brbljavka the magpie
- Outfits for the players, a sticker album and diplomas to print
- Tap the word twice to hear it slowly
- For parents: easy mode for ages 3-4, a daily time limit, a weekly report
- Better on tablets
</en-US>
