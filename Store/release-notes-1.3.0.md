# Release notes 1.3.0 (versionCode 6)

Paste each block into the matching language in Play Console (max 500 characters each).
Before this release: the Arabic words must be checked by a native speaker (`Store/arabic-review.md`)
and the test APK tried on a phone.

<en-US>
- New: Arabic words for Bosnian-speaking children, with Latin letters below
- New world "Kind Words": selam, thank you, here you are, sorry, bismillah, mashallah
- The story of Brbljavka the magpie: catch her at the end of every world
- Sticker album: a full page gives a new player
- Tap the word to hear it again, tap twice to hear it slowly
- For parents: easy mode for ages 3-4, a daily time limit, a weekly report
- Pepi talks more, and progress is saved more safely
</en-US>

<hr>
- Novo: arapske riječi za djecu koja govore bosanski, s latinicom ispod
- Novi svijet „Lijepe riječi“: selam, hvala, izvoli, oprosti, bismillah, mašallah
- Priča o svraci Brbljavki: uhvati je na kraju svakog svijeta
- Album naljepnica: puna stranica donosi novog igrača
- Dodirni riječ da je čuješ ponovo, dodirni dvaput da je čuješ polako
- Za roditelje: lagani način za 3-4 godine, dnevno ograničenje vremena, izvještaj
- Pepi više priča, a napredak se sigurnije čuva
</hr>
