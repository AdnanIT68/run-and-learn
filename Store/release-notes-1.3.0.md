# Release notes 1.3.0 (versionCode 7; build 6 bio u internom testu)

Paste each block into the matching language in Play Console (max 500 characters each).
Arabic checked (17. 11. 2026). Build 6 is in internal testing since 17. 11.; try it on a phone
(diploma and worksheet sharing too) before production.

<en-US>
- New: Arabic words for Bosnian-speaking children, with Latin letters
- Up to 4 children on one phone, each with their own progress
- 3 new worlds: Kind Words, Colours and Shapes, Body and Clothes
- A read-aloud picture book in every world
- Mini-games, letter tracing, adding and taking away up to ten
- Outfits, a sticker album and a word of the day
- For parents: diplomas and worksheets to print, easy mode, time limit, report
- Better on tablets
</en-US>
